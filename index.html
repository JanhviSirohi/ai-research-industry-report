  • Funding is concentrated in synthetic users + AI moderation, but practitioner sentiment grew more skeptical between 2024 and 2026. Listen Labs ($69M Jan 2026, $500M valuation) S009, S010, S173, Outset ($30M Dec 2025) S005, S163, S168, Aaru ($1B headline at sub-$10M ARR Dec 2025) S055, S178, S179, Simile ($100M Feb 2026) S059, S060, S058 all closed material rounds.   • The hardest XR-specific finding: no SaaS qual tool natively ingests 200 Hz binocular gaze + biosignals S402, S442; Apple Vision Pro does not expose raw gaze coordinates S422, S491; Pico 4 Ultra dropped eye tracking S485, S486, S434; Tobii–iMotions ended their commercial partnership. 4. B2C vs B2B and regional cuts

Figure 2 — B2C vs B2B fit per AI-research category

Audience-fit applied: B2C row = consumer XR studies; B2B = enterprise XR pilots (Pico's 200 districts, Varjo military, surgical training).

The B2C/B2B asymmetry is sharp. AI moderation works in B2C only when the study is structured (feedback at scale, screener-led, post-launch sentiment); for discovery it fails on both sides — practitioners report disengagement from the AI orb S251, S255. Synthetic users invert the pattern: they are publicly disavowed for B2C UX work (Hall, Spool, Merholz, Rosala/Moran) S241, S293, S250, S246 yet Evidenza's profitable pattern shows synthetic-CMO/CFO personas can serve B2B media planning S057, S198, S199. Qual synthesis is the rare both-sides win; XR's small-N enterprise pilots benefit because the unit of analysis is the quote, not the aggregate S287, S033, S115 C3.

Regional reality is constraining and tightening, not loosening. The EU AI Act's GPAI obligations have applied since 2 Aug 2025 S366, S367, S368; the high-risk Annex III deadline of 2 Aug 2026 stands after the Digital Omnibus failed in trilogue on 28 April 2026 (two days before this report) S369, S370, S371, S376. EDPB Opinion 28/2024 governs synthetic-user training data S372, S373, S374, S375 C3. China's three-pillar cross-border framework (Security Assessment, SCC filing, PIP Certification effective 1 Jan 2026) is now operationally complete — there is no longer ambiguity about whether a route exists, only which one applies S341, S342, S343, S346, S347, S345, S350. AI-Content Labeling Measures + GB 45438-2025 (effective 1 Sept 2025) require implicit and explicit labels on AI-generated text, image, audio, video and virtual scenes — directly relevant to synthetic transcripts and stimuli in any China-facing deliverable S358, S359, S360. CCPA ADMT regulations are effective 1 Jan 2026 with ADMT-specific obligations binding 1 Jan 2027 S381, S382, S384, S380 C3.

Mandarin is solvable on text; Cantonese is not. Per SuperCLUE Feb 2026, ByteDance Doubao, Qwen, DeepSeek, GLM and Kimi sit in the global top tier on Mandarin; the Chinese frontier trails GPT/Gemini by ~6 points at 10–15% the cost S385, S386, S387, S393, S395. But "Do Chinese models speak Chinese languages?" (arXiv 2504.00289) and "How Well Do LLMs Handle Cantonese?" (NAACL Findings 2025) document a systematic Cantonese performance gap, particularly with the Mandarin/English/Cantonese code-switching that defines HK fieldwork S388, S389, S390, S391. GLM-ASR-Nano-2512 and fine-tuned Whisper close part of it; treat any Western "100+ languages" tool as unverified for Cantonese until tested against AISHELL6 / HKCanto-Eval S099, S459, S392, S391 C3.

5. Hardware + XR-specific implications

XR research resists AI substitution at two layers the SaaS-qual category was never built for: multimodal capture and long-cycle field studies. No SaaS qual tool today natively ingests 200 Hz binocular gaze + EEG + GSR + hand kinematics; they are built around a single video stream of a participant's face S402, S442, S034. Tobii Pro Lab's PLOF 2.0 export remains the de facto bridge to Dovetail/Marvin/Notably — a CSV bridge, not a true integration S443, S488, S442 C2. The Tobii–iMotions commercial partnership ended, fragmenting the space further S400, S401, S440. Apple Vision Pro does not expose raw gaze coordinates to developers (confirmed by the iTrace UIST/arXiv 2025 paper, which had to substitute pinch/dwell/gamepad proxies) S422, S491; Pico 4 Ultra dropped eye tracking entirely for cost; only Pico 4 Enterprise, Vision Pro at the developer level via workarounds, and Varjo XR-4 (200 Hz, 1° accuracy, with the new XR_VARJO_eye_tracking_analytics OpenXR extension) keep raw gaze open S434, S485, S486, S438, S439, S440 C3. Long-cycle field studies — "does this glasses form factor still get used in week 3?" — cannot be simulated; synthetic users cannot fabricate adherence drop-off or the texture of week-3 boredom S250, S246, S456 C3.

The credible 2026 stack for a Chinese-parent XR research function is four platforms, not one, drawn from Phase 5 evidence: (1) capture — Tobii Pro Lab or iMotions Lab as the primary multimodal recorder, plus Pupil Labs Neon / hmd-eyes for retrofit on Quest/Pico S442, S402, S403, S405; (2) in-headset runtime — WorldViz Vizard 8 + SightLab VR Pro 2 (80+ templates, LLM-driven AI agents released Nov 2024) or build on the academic Toolkit for VR Data Collection + StudyVR pattern over OpenXR S444, S445, S419, S410; (3) transcription — iFlytek SR502 hardware or Alibaba Fun-ASR self-hosted for confidential Mandarin/Cantonese sessions, Whisper or Speechmatics on-prem as global fallback S074, S458, S099, S106, S457; (4) synthesis — Volcengine (Doubao, ByteDance) for in-China workloads, Condens for EU residency, Dovetail with workspace isolation for global S465, S466, S107, S108, S033. Avoid Synthetic Users for any hardware decision. S196, S250, S241 C2

6. Counter-narrative: what isn't working

The 2024–2026 practitioner record does not support replacing primary research with AI-led methods for any decision a hardware team would care about. Synthetic users are publicly disavowed by NN/g (Rosala, Moran) S250, S251, Erika Hall ("unethical, indefensible, unnecessary") S241, S297, Jared Spool ("absolutely the wrong direction") S293, Peter Merholz, Indi Young ("ChatGPT does not understand meaning") S242, S243, S296, Pavel Samsonov ("the LLM can't buy your product") S244, S245, Niloufar Salehi (UC Berkeley: "superficial, stereotype-based") S246, Matt Duignan (Microsoft: "the next big myth") S258, S291, Emma Travis (Speero) S248 and Greg Nudelman S247 C3. User Interviews' SoUR 2025 (n=485) records 49% negative sentiment on UXR's future, +26 pts YoY; 67% pessimistic on careers, +21 pts; 11% personally laid off; 21% saw researcher layoffs at their company S285, S116. GRIT 2025 puts brand-side researcher satisfaction with GenAI at 13%, with data-quality concerns up 40% YoY S284, S302, S303, S304, S277 C3. Patent reality is harsh: Outset, Listen Labs, Strella, Genway, Conveo, Synthetic Users, Aaru, Yabble, Evidenza, Fairgen, Notably, Marvin, Reduct, Dovetail and dscout return no substantive issued or published patent on their core mechanism — moats are data, distribution and prompt design, not invention S152, S153, S196, S046, S256 C3. Wondering shut down 23 Sept 2025 (first prominent AI-research failure of the cycle) S180, S181, S032; Aaru's $1B headline sits over sub-$10M ARR S055, S179, S178; Medallia is being handed to lenders Apr 2026 with $5.1B equity wipeout, AI-substitution risk explicitly cited S214, S216, S217 C3. The honest picture is: AI synthesis is a real productivity gain at the inside of the workflow S287, S033; AI moderation is in production for structured B2C work only S298, S172, S299; and synthetic respondents are the highest-variance bet in the category, ahead of outcomes by a wide margin S256, S307.

7. Appendix — Methodology, confidence framework, sources

Methodology

This report synthesises six independent evidence streams compiled across April 2026 by a single research function: a vendor matrix of ~55 vendors against eight functions; a USPTO/EPO/WIPO patent and IP scan of ~55 filings; a 24-month funding and M&A trace covering ~111 sources; a practitioner-sentiment review across blogs, podcasts, conferences and three industry surveys (User Interviews SoUR, Maze, Lyssna); an analyst, regulatory and language/China ecosystem layer (Greenbook GRIT, ESOMAR, Forrester, Gartner, IDC, Quirks, MRS); and an XR/hardware-specific layer covering capture, in-headset runtime, transcription and multimodal sync. Each load-bearing claim was triangulated across at least two source classes before promotion to C3; C2 indicates practitioner-corroborated single-source-class evidence; C1 denotes vendor self-report only.

Confidence framework

C1 — Vendor self-report only (vendor blog, marketing site, founder LinkedIn, press release). C2 — Practitioner-corroborated by at least one independent practitioner write-up, podcast discussion, conference talk, or analyst note. C3 — Multiple independent source classes confirm; e.g. patents + funding + practitioner sentiment, or analyst + regulator + primary user data.

Limitations

  • No native CNIPA Chinese-language patent search was performed — Chinese-language research-methodology patents from Pico, ByteDance, Tencent, Alibaba, NetEase and iFlytek may exist but were not retrievable via Google Patents/USPTO/WIPO English search.
  • EPO Espacenet listings could not be paged via WebFetch in the patent run; deeper pull recommended for European-only Tobii, Bosch, Apple filings.
  • Several vendor revenue figures (Aaru sub-$10M ARR; Dovetail $4.9M 2024) come from third-party trackers (GetLatka) and are not audited.
  • Practitioner sentiment is dominated by English-language UX/MR sources; the Chinese-language practitioner debate on AI moderators / synthetic users is a known gap and was flagged at C1 rather than smoothed.
  • "100+ language" vendor claims have not been benchmarked against Chinese-consumer ground truth in this report — the academic record (arXiv 2504.00289, NAACL 2025 Cantonese, HKCanto-Eval) is used as the credible substitute.
  • Forecasts deliberately stop at 18 months per Phase 1 scope; longer-horizon projections were excluded.
